Project Overview

Resource Technologies Systems Ltd was engaged to design and implement integrated ELV systems for a private residential development completed in 2024.

The project emphasized structured infrastructure, clean system separation, and long-term serviceability rather than short-term installation speed

Systems Implemented

  • Structured Cabling Infrastructure
  • CCTV Surveillance System
  • Access Control Provisions
  • Fire Detection Provisions

Engineering Approach

  1. Review of architectural layouts and coordination with other trades
  2. Standards-based cable routing and containment strategy
  3. Proper zoning and device positioning for safety and coverage
  4. Clear labeling, documentation, and handover readiness

Delivery & Outcome

The project was delivered in line with design intent and functional requirements. All systems were tested and verified, with infrastructure designed to accommodate future expansion or system upgrades without major rework.

Why This Project Matters

This project reflects our core philosophy: engineering clarity, standards compliance, and long-term system performance over volume-based execution.
